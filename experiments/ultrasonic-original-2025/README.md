# Original ultrasonic sketch (July 2025)

`Drain_Warning_System.ino` is the very first version of the drain monitor: a bare
HC-SR04 aimed at the open drain, NewPing, six LEDs on fixed distance thresholds, no WiFi.
It came from the old Arduino IDE sketchbook (`Dropbox\Arduino\Drain_Warning_System`) and
was moved here in September 2026 when the project was consolidated.

History only. Do not flash it and do not treat its pin numbers as current; the deployed
pin map is in `firmware/src/StJoesDrainMonitor.ino`.

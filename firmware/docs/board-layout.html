St. Joes Drain Monitor — perfboard layout + power

Power: 5 V USB wall-wart powers the ESP32; an MT3608 boost (set to 12 V) off the board's 5 V pin makes the 12 V rail for the buzzer + ring LED. Switches: logic-level N-MOSFET (2N7000 or RFP30N06LE) — gate from the GPIO, 100 kΩ gate pull-down, no base resistor. Firmware active-HIGH, unchanged.

Power flow (USB 5 V → ESP32; 5 V → MT3608 boost → 12 V rail)

 5V USB wall-wart ─▶ ESP32 micro-USB ─▶ powers the board (5V rail / onboard 3.3V)
                                          │
                       ESP32 5V pin ──────┴─▶ [MT3608 boost → 12V] ─▶ 12V RAIL ─┬─▶ buzzer +
                                                (trim pot to 12.0 V!)            └─▶ ring LED +

 GND (one common): USB − = ESP32 GND = MT3608 IN−/OUT− = both MOSFET sources = gate pull-downs

Single supply = no headaches: only USB feeds the board, so there's no source-coexistence issue (no Schottky/diode-OR needed), and the boost shares USB ground so the common ground is automatic. Front-panel bar LEDs/lamp stay GPIO-driven (3.3 V), unaffected.

⚠ Set the MT3608 to 12.0 V before connecting the ring LED. Out of the box the pot is at a random spot and the output can be anything up to ~28 V — power the boost from 5 V, turn the pot, and meter OUT to 12.0 V first. The buzzer (3–24 V) is forgiving; the 12 V ring LED is not.

Gone vs. the earlier plan: no 12 V adapter, barrel jack, buck, or Schottky. The MT3608 replaces all of that. MOSFET pinouts: 2N7000 = S–G–D (TO-92), RFP30N06LE = G–D–S (TO-220, tab=Drain).

Bottom-half placement detail (the empty area below the ESP32)

Left→right: button + Q2 (by GPIO 32/25), buzzer + Q1 (by GPIO 27/GND), MT3608 boost (fed from the 5V pin). Two wire-link buses along the bottom: a 12 V bus (from boost OUT+) and a GND bus.

MOSFET pinouts: 2N7000 (TO-92) = S–G–D; RFP30N06LE (TO-220) = G–D–S, tab=Drain. The 2N7000's small TO-92 fits the board best for these tiny loads.
